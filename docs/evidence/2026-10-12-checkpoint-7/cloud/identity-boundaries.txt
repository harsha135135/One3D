# What the deployment user (one3d-deployer) can and cannot reach, 2026-10-12 (read-only calls)
caller: arn:aws:iam::<account>:user/one3d-deployer

## OneCreations (must be refused)
Amplify app of OneCreations (get-app): AccessDeniedException
onecreations-media-<account> bucket (list): AccessDenied
OneCreations budget (describe): AccessDeniedException
EC2 outside ap-south-1 (us-east-1 describe-instances): UnauthorizedOperation, explicit deny in the identity policy (outside ap-south-1)

## One3D (must be allowed)
one3d artifacts bucket (list): allowed
ECR one3d-backend (describe images): allowed
SSM /one3d/demo parameters (names only): allowed

  OneCreations-Monthly-Budget	10.0	USD	MONTHLY
